Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Eagle Eats helps UNT students browse dining hall menus, track nutrition, and manage meal plan information. This policy describes what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Data We Collect

  • Account: Display name and session state after you sign in through UNT's official meal plan portal. We do not store your UNT password.
  • Dining activity: Meals you log, nutrition goals, favorites, and settings stored on your device.
  • Location (optional): Used only to show nearest dining halls when you grant permission.
  • Photos (optional): Only when you add a photo to a menu item review.
  • Check-ins (optional): Display name and dining hall sent to our backend to show activity counts.
  • Feedback & reports: Content you submit or report.

Third Parties

  • UNT portal: Authentication happens on UNT's website in a secure web view.
  • Supabase: Menu data and optional check-ins are stored on Supabase.
  • UNT dining websites: Public menu data aggregated by our backend for display in the app.

We do not sell your personal data. Third parties must protect data consistent with this policy.

Retention & Deletion

Most data is stored on your device. You can delete your account from Settings in the app, which clears local credentials and session data. Server-side check-ins may persist until our retention cleanup runs (typically within 7 days). Contact us to request deletion of other server data tied to you.

Children

Eagle Eats is intended for UNT students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
