About Mean Eats

Eating on campus shouldn't mean juggling five different websites, guessing what's open, or doing mental math on your swipes and Flex balance. Mean Eats brings everything UNT dining into one thoughtfully designed iOS app.

Whether you're a freshman exploring Kerr Hall for the first time or a senior making every swipe count, Mean Eats helps you browse menus, track nutrition, find open halls, and stay on top of your meal plan — without the friction.

Sign in through UNT's official meal plan portal to see your Dining Swipes and Flex balance with auto-refresh. Or tap Continue as Guest to explore menus, maps, and nutrition info with no account needed. Mean Eats never stores your UNT password.
