# Eagle Eats Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 2026

Eagle Eats helps UNT students browse dining hall menus, track nutrition, and manage meal plan information.

## Data We Collect

- **Account:** Display name and session state after you sign in through UNT's official meal plan portal. We do not store your UNT password.
- **Dining activity:** Meals you log, nutrition goals, favorites, and settings stored on your device.
- **Location (optional):** Used only to show nearest dining halls when you grant permission.
- **Photos (optional):** Only when you add a photo to a menu item review.
- **Check-ins (optional):** Display name and dining hall sent to our backend to show activity counts.
- **Feedback & reports:** Content you submit or report.

## Third Parties

- **UNT portal:** Authentication on UNT's website.
- **Supabase:** Menu data and optional check-ins.
- **UNT dining websites:** Public menu data aggregated by our backend.

We do not sell your personal data.

## Your Choices

Delete your account in the app (Settings) to clear local data.
