# Eagle Eats Support

Need help with the Eagle Eats app? We're here for UNT students using the app to browse dining halls, track nutrition, and view meal plan info.

## Contact

**Email:** [va12345bhavg@gmail.com](mailto:va12345bhavg@gmail.com)

We aim to respond within 2–3 business days.

## Common topics

- **Sign in:** Use your UNT EUID and password on the official UNT meal plan portal inside the app.
- **Meal plan balance:** Pull to refresh on the Meal Plan tab after signing in.
- **Delete account:** Settings → Delete Account (removes local data from your device).
- **Privacy:** See our [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}).

## App information

- **App name:** Eagle Eats
- **Platform:** iOS
